Visual direction

Warm paper, forest green actions, editorial serif headings, compact utility labels, and generous spacing. All branding, copy, questions, and graphics are original. System fonts and inline vector graphics keep the prototype self-contained. Student data and score ranges are fictional.

Production test specification

The prototype is a shortened interaction demo, not a full digital SAT simulation. Production needs a setup screen, separate Reading & Writing and Math sections, timed modules, module-end review, breaks, locked submitted modules, and a results summary. Configure question counts, timing, accommodations and routing from a versioned test definition; verify current format against official College Board documentation before claiming fidelity. The demo’s mixed skills, five-minute timer and pause control are not exam rules.

Use a server-authoritative deadline and autosave; restore answers after reconnect. Keep answer keys inaccessible until submission. Add accessible math rendering and a supported calculator integration. Authentication, backend, score model, adaptive engine, full question bank and official testing tools are outside this design prototype.

States and accessibility

AreaRequired production states
DashboardLoading, new student/diagnostic prompt, no tasks, unavailable progress/retry, goal reached. Hide numeric estimates until sufficient evidence exists.
PracticeUnselected, selected, correct, incorrect, hint, session complete, empty bank, save error. Record first attempts separately from retries.
TestReady, running, low time, offline/autosave pending, submission, expired. Announce time warnings at thresholds rather than each second.
ReviewCorrect, incorrect, unanswered, no attempts, reflection saving/saved/error. Preserve unsaved notes across question switches.
AccessibilityTarget WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 text, 3:1 essential control boundaries. Darken decorative borders where needed for control identification. Use native radio groups in production with arrow-key support, visible focus, text outcome labels, 44px touch targets, descriptive navigator names, 200% zoom, and focus restoration after route/dialog changes. Prototype buttons support keyboard activation; a full accessibility audit is still required.

Developer handoff and acceptance

Self-contained HTML/CSS/JavaScript; no installation or external dependencies. Split the contracts above into components in your chosen framework. Suggested routes: /dashboard, /practice, /test/:sessionId, /review/:attemptId. Prototype uses hash routes to work locally.

QA target sizes: 1440×1000, 834×1194, 390×844, and 320px wide. Verify subject switching, hints/check, timer, answer persistence, flags, unanswered submission, expiry, submitted review, note save/reload, keyboard-only use and zoom. Browser visual validation and assistive-technology auditing remain required before production.

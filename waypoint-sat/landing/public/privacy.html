Privacy Policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

The short version. We collect only what we need to run SAT practice and tutor matching. We do not sell your data or show ads. Other users see only your first name and last initial until a connection is accepted, and never see your ZIP code or exact location. You can ask us to delete your account.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account: email address, password (stored only as a one-way hash), and whether you signed up as a student, a tutor or both.
  • Profile: your full name and ZIP code. Students may add grade and subjects they want help with. Tutors add a LinkedIn profile link, school or university, subjects, a short bio, availability, travel distance and whether they are taking students.
  • Tutoring activity: requests and the short notes in them, whether they were accepted or declined, sessions a tutor logs (date, length, note) and whether a student confirmed them, reports and blocks, and certificates.
  • Study data: your answers, tests, scores, notes, settings and study plan.
  • AI inputs: questions you ask the assistant and any typed work or handwriting photos you submit to the AI tools.

Information collected automatically

  • A sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in, and for visitors who are not signed in an anonymous study cookie. Both are strictly necessary for the service to work.
  • Your browser’s local storage, which keeps a temporary copy of your study progress so it is not lost if you go offline.
  • Basic technical logs (such as request errors) and the time you were last active. We use activity timestamps for administration and keeping the service healthy. We do not run advertising or cross-site tracking.

2. How we use information

  • To run Prep Orbit: sign you in, save progress, and give you practice and AI help.
  • To match students with tutors, send invitation and notification emails, record and confirm sessions, and issue and verify certificates.
  • To keep people safe: review reports, prevent abuse and misuse, enforce our Terms, and respond to legal requests.
  • To maintain and improve the service, including checking the quality of practice questions.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Who we share information with

We use a small number of service providers who process information for us:

  • Hosting and database: Railway, which hosts the application and its PostgreSQL database in the United States.
  • Email: Resend, which delivers sign-up, password-reset and tutoring emails.
  • AI: OpenAI, which receives the text and images you submit to AI features in order to generate a response. We ask the provider not to store these requests.
  • Graphing calculator: the Desmos calculator is embedded from desmos.com. When you open it, your browser connects to Desmos, which has its own privacy policy.

We may also share information if required by law or legal process, to protect the safety of a person (including reporting suspected child exploitation to the authorities), or to protect our rights and the service. If Prep Orbit is ever transferred to another organization, your information may be transferred with it, and we will tell you first.

5. Children and teens

Prep Orbit is for people 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it. Teens under 18 should use tutoring only with a parent or guardian’s knowledge. Parents and guardians may ask us to review, correct or delete a teen’s information.

6. How long we keep information

We keep account, study and tutoring information while your account exists. When you ask us to delete your account we delete or de-identify your profile, study data and connections within a reasonable time, except where we must keep something to resolve a safety report, prevent abuse, or meet a legal obligation. A certificate that has already been issued may be kept so it can still be verified, unless you ask us to remove it. Backups are overwritten on a normal rotation.

7. Your choices

  • Update or correct your profile at any time in the app. Tutors can pause new requests by turning off “open to new students.”
  • Export your study progress from the app footer.
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete your information, or to delete your account, using the contact below. We may need to verify that the request comes from you.
  • You can clear cookies and local storage in your browser, but you will be signed out and may lose unsynced progress.

8. Security

We use encrypted connections, hash passwords, limit the information shown to other users as described above, and rate-limit sensitive requests. No system is perfectly secure, so use a strong, unique password and tell us right away if you think your account was accessed by someone else.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. If a change is significant we will post a notice in the app or email you. The date above shows when it was last updated.

The short version. Prep Orbit AI gives you free SAT practice and AI study help, and lets students ask volunteer tutors nearby for help. It is free. Tutors are volunteers we do not background-check, so use the safety steps on our Safety page. Certificates recognize effort; they do not guarantee admission. Please read the full terms below.

1. What Prep Orbit AI is

Prep Orbit AI (“Prep Orbit,” “we,” “us”) provides (a) free SAT practice questions, tests and study tools, some of which use artificial intelligence; (b) a directory that lets students find and send email invitations to volunteer tutors in their area; (c) a log where tutors record teaching sessions that students confirm; and (d) certificates that recognize volunteer tutoring hours. By creating an account or using the service you agree to these terms and to our Privacy Policy and Safety page.

Prep Orbit is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by College Board. “SAT” is a registered trademark of College Board. Our practice questions are original and are not official SAT questions.

2. Who can use it

  • You must be at least 13 years old. We do not knowingly allow children under 13 to have accounts.
  • If you are under 18, a parent or guardian should know that you use Prep Orbit and must agree before you meet any tutor in person.
  • Give accurate information, keep your password private, and use one account per person. You are responsible for activity on your account.

3. The service is free

Prep Orbit and tutoring through it are free. Tutors must not ask for or accept money, gifts or other payment from students or families for help arranged through Prep Orbit. If we add optional paid features in the future, we will tell you first and they will not change what is free today.

4. Tutoring is between independent people

Tutors are independent volunteers. They are not our employees, agents or contractors, and we do not supervise, schedule or attend tutoring sessions. Please understand the following before you connect with anyone:

  • We do not run background checks on tutors, students or anyone else.
  • A tutor’s school, LinkedIn page, subjects and bio are entered by the tutor. We do not verify them. A “Reviewed” badge means only that an administrator looked at the profile at some point; it is not a background check, a credential check or an endorsement.
  • Meeting happens offline, outside Prep Orbit. You decide whether, when and where to meet, and you do so at your own risk. Follow the steps on the Safety page.
  • We do not guarantee that any tutor will accept a request, be qualified, be available, or be suitable for you.

5. Community guidelines

When you sign up, you agree to these rules. We may remove content, hide profiles, end connections or close accounts that break them.

  • Be respectful. No harassment, bullying, hate, threats, sexual content or sexual contact of any kind with a minor, or anything illegal.
  • Never ask for or accept payment for tutoring arranged through Prep Orbit.
  • Do not ask students for personal details such as home address, school schedule, photos or social media accounts, and do not try to learn a student’s identity before they accept a connection.
  • Do not impersonate anyone, give false information about your identity, school or experience, or create accounts to get around a block or a removal.
  • Do not log sessions that did not happen, ask a student to confirm sessions that did not happen, or otherwise inflate hours.
  • Do not scrape, copy at scale, resell, attack or overload the service, or try to access other people’s accounts or data.

6. Certificates

After a tutor has 100 hours that students have confirmed, an administrator may issue a certificate that bears the tutor’s full name, hours, dates and an official stamp, with a link anyone can use to verify it. Certificates recognize participation and contribution. They are not academic credit, a professional credential, a recommendation or a guarantee of college admission, scholarships or any other outcome.

Hours are recorded by tutors and confirmed by students; we do not independently observe them. We may decline to issue, or may revoke, a certificate if we believe hours or details are false, if a guideline was broken, or if there was a mistake. A certificate’s verification page is public to anyone who has the link.

7. AI features

Parts of Prep Orbit use AI to write explanations, check work, answer questions and help create practice questions. AI can be wrong. Check important answers, and do not rely on AI output as professional, medical, legal or admissions advice. Do not enter passwords, financial details or other sensitive information into AI tools. What you type or upload to an AI feature is sent to our AI provider to produce a response, as described in the Privacy Policy. We do not guarantee any score improvement.
